About Heart Disease

Key facts and risk factors you should know

❤️

What is Heart Disease?

Heart disease refers to conditions affecting the heart's structure and function, including coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, and heart defects. It is the leading cause of death globally.

⚠️

Risk Factors

High blood pressure, high cholesterol, smoking, diabetes, obesity, physical inactivity, and family history are primary risk factors. Early detection is critical for prevention.

🤖

CNN-based Diagnosis

Our 1D CNN treats 13 clinical features as a 1D sequence, enabling deep feature extraction via convolutional filters — achieving high accuracy comparable to medical baselines.

🏥

Dataset Source

We use the UCI Heart Disease dataset (available on Kaggle). It contains 303 patient records with 13 clinical attributes and a binary target (0=No Disease, 1=Disease).

⚕️

Disclaimer

This tool is for educational and research purposes only. It is not a medical device and should not replace professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified physician.

📋

Clinical Parameters

The model uses: Age, Sex, Chest Pain Type, Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Blood Sugar, ECG, Max Heart Rate, Exercise Angina, ST Depression, Slope, Vessels, and Thalassemia.
